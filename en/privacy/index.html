Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Information on the processing of personal data under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) for anyone who visits this website or contacts Dr Matjaz Vuga through the contact details provided.

Data controller

The data controller is Dr Matjaz Vuga, orthopaedic surgeon, registered with the Medical Association of Gorizia no. 1327. Privacy contact: matjaz.vuga@asugi.sanita.fvg.it.

Data processed

This website has no contact forms, creates no accounts and does not collect data directly. The server hosting it automatically records some technical browsing data (IP address, date and time, requested page). If you choose to contact the doctor by WhatsApp, email or phone, we will process the data you provide (name, contact details, message content). Please do not send detailed clinical information in the first contact: you can provide it during the visit.

Purposes and legal basis

Ensuring the operation and security of the site (legitimate interest, Art. 6.1.f GDPR); replying to your requests and managing appointments (pre-contractual steps at your request, Art. 6.1.b GDPR). If your message contains health data, we process it only to answer your request, on the basis of your explicit consent given by contacting us voluntarily (Art. 9.2.a GDPR).

Recipients

Data may be processed by the website hosting provider and by the services you choose to contact us with: WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd) and your email provider, under their own policies. Data is never disclosed or sold.

Transfers outside the EU

Some providers may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Retention

Technical logs are kept by the hosting provider only as long as strictly necessary for security and operation. Messages are kept as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, and object to its processing (Arts. 15-22 GDPR) by writing to matjaz.vuga@asugi.sanita.fvg.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies or other tracking tools, no analytics or advertising services, and loads no content from third-party sites (fonts and images are hosted on the site itself). For this reason no consent banner is required.

External links

The site links to external services (Google Maps, WhatsApp, ASUGI, the Medical Association of Gorizia). When you open them you leave this site and their own privacy notices apply.

Changes

This notice may be updated; the date of the last update is shown at the top.
