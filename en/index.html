About me

Specialist in accurate diagnosis and tailored treatment of bones, joints and sports injuries (knee, hip, shoulder, foot/ankle and general traumatology). Offers a personalised, shared care pathway: from accurate diagnosis to full functional recovery.

Education

  • Degree in Medicine and Surgery
  • Specialisation in Orthopaedics and Traumatology
  • Registered with the Gorizia Medical Board, no. 1327

Specialties

A thorough clinical assessment and a shared care plan, from diagnosis to recovery.

  • 01

    Knee

    Meniscus and ligament injuries, osteoarthritis, anterior knee pain.

  • 02

    Hip

    Osteoarthritis, femoroacetabular impingement, hip pain in adults and athletes.

  • 03

    Shoulder

    Rotator cuff tendinopathy, instability, frozen shoulder.

  • 04

    Sports medicine

    Sports injuries and overuse, prevention and return to activity.

  • 05

    Foot and ankle

    Sprains, bunions, metatarsalgia and plantar fasciitis.

  • 06

    Traumatology

    Fractures and traumatic injuries: assessment, treatment and follow-up.

  • 07

    Intra-articular injections

    Targeted treatments for joint pain: platelet-rich plasma (PRP), hyaluronic acid and anti-inflammatory injections.
